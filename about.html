INSTITUTIONAL NEUTRALITY & EXCELLENCE

Connecting Nigeria's Largest Fixed Income Dealing Members & Asset Managers

Krosk Partners Limited (KPL) is an institutional inter-dealer brokerage intermediary operating under the regulatory architecture of the FMDQ OTC Securities Exchange. We act as an independent conduit facilitating seamless trade executions between licensed Dealing Banks and premier buy-side institutions, including Pension Fund Administrators (PFAs), asset management firms, and insurance treasuries.

Operating at the very epicentre of the wholesale secondary market, KPL bridges the gap between commercial bank treasuries, discount houses, and institutional fund managers. By maintaining strict pre-trade anonymity and synthesizing continuous two-way quote flow, our registered brokers enable market participants to execute substantial block transactions without destabilizing price slippage.

FMDQ OTC REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

Governance, Compliance & Market Ethics

Krosk Partners Limited operates in strict accordance with the rules and guidelines governing Associate Member Inter-Dealer Brokers.

⚖️ // ABSOLUTE NEUTRALITY

Neutral Intermediation

We act solely in an agency capacity as an intermediary broker. KPL never takes proprietary principal positions against client orders, ensuring absolute neutrality and zero conflict of interest.

🔒 // DISCRETION GUARANTEE

Pre-Trade Confidentiality

Rigorous Chinese walls and confidentiality protocols protect counterparty mandates from premature market exposure, preventing front-running and adverse price slippage.

📋 // INTEGRITY & AML

KYC & AML Compliance

Full compliance with Nigerian financial regulations and international AML/CFT standards. All institutional counterparties undergo exhaustive onboarding verification prior to trade intermediation.
