DESK 02 — SOVEREIGN DISCOUNT INSTRUMENTS

Treasury Bill Instruments Desk

Providing institutional access to short-term sovereign debt securities issued by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) on behalf of the Federal Government.

Sold at discount and maturing at par value (₦100), Treasury Bills represent the risk-free benchmark for Nigerian money market yields. Our desk tracks Central Bank Primary Market Auctions (PMA), Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) debit cycles, and secondary market liquidity to secure the highest effective yields for institutional buyers.

DESK 03 — SHORT-TERM LIQUIDITY & CREDIT

Money Market Instruments Desk

Optimizing overnight, short-tenor, and corporate money market yields across Nigeria's banking and institutional investment sectors.

Our Money Market desk provides comprehensive access to prime Commercial Papers (CPs) issued by top conglomerates, negotiable Certificates of Deposit (CDs), Money Market Mutual Fund instruments, and bilateral Repurchase Agreements (Repos) / Open Buy Back (OBB) facilities.

✓ Commercial Papers: Tenors from 90 to 270 days offering attractive credit spreads over NTBs.
✓ Repo & OBB: Intermediating overnight and term liquidity between cash-surplus and cash-deficit banks.
✓ Certificates of Deposit: Customized term deposit paper structured to corporate cash cycles.
